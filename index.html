Why the Ready for School Experience?

At Manners on Point School of Etiquette, we believe preparing children for school goes beyond buying uniforms and books. Children also need confidence, good manners, effective communication skills and the ability to build positive relationships.

The Ready for School Experience has been thoughtfully designed to equip children with practical life skills that will help them transition confidently into a new school year.

Participants will be able to:

  • Walk confidently into a new school environment.
  • Introduce themselves with confidence.
  • Build respectful friendships.
  • Communicate clearly and politely.
  • Demonstrate good manners in everyday situations.
  • Adapt confidently to new school routines, including boarding school life.
  • Represent themselves, their families and their schools with confidence and dignity.

Meet Your Trainer

Abenaa Antwiwaa Adusei

Founder & Lead Trainer
Manners on Point School of Etiquette

Abenaa Antwiwaa Adusei is a Certified Etiquette Consultant, Elocutionist and Founder of Manners on Point School of Etiquette.

She is passionate about equipping children, teenagers and adults with the confidence, character and life skills they need to thrive in every environment.

Through engaging, practical and interactive training, she has impacted more than 8,000 lives and worked with over 20 corporate organisations, helping individuals present themselves with confidence, professionalism and grace.

She believes every child deserves the opportunity to develop the confidence to succeed, the character to lead and the manners to leave a lasting positive impression.
